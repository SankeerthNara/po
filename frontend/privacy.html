privacy policy.

Last updated 25 October 2026

This is the personal website of Sankeerth Nara (“I”, “me”). It collects as little as it can. This page says what is collected, why, and how to have it removed.

Contact form

When you send a message, the name, email address and message you type are delivered to my inbox through FormSubmit. I use them only to reply to you. I don't sell them, share them or add you to any mailing list.

Analytics

The site may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It records things like your approximate location, device, browser and the pages you view, using cookies. It does not tell me who you are. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies.

Stored on your device

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Organisation logos load from GitHub. Their own privacy policies apply to that data.

Your rights

To see, correct or delete anything you've sent me, email sankeerthnara@gmail.com and I'll act on it within 30 days.
